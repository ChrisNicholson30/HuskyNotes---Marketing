# Husky Notes — Brand & Content Overview

The source of truth for **what Husky Notes is, who it’s for, and how it speaks.** Use this when writing copy, designing assets, or briefing anyone on the product.

-----

## One-liner

> Markdown notes you’ll love, sync you can trust.

A native, open-source note-taking app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. **Obsidian’s openness, Bear’s craft, a signature Blue Husky theme.**

-----

## Positioning

Husky Notes sits deliberately between two reference points:

|Reference   |What we borrow                                                                       |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|**Obsidian**|Open, plain-text soul — notes are just Markdown, nothing trapped, nothing proprietary|
|**Bear**    |Effortless reliability and craft — beautiful, themeable, “just works”                |

The wedge: most note apps force a trade-off between *open data* and *polish*. Husky refuses the trade-off — your notes are always plain Markdown **and** the editor is a joy.

**Core promise:** Your data is yours, forever — made enforceable by open source + first-class `.md` export.

-----

## Audience

- Apple-platform users (iOS/iPadOS 18+, macOS 15+) who write in Markdown.
- People wary of lock-in — they want their notes portable and auditable.
- Obsidian-curious users who want native feel without the web-view friction.
- Privacy-conscious writers — no account, no tracking, no server.

-----

## Messaging pillars

1. **Markdown at heart** — every note body is plain CommonMark + GFM. Live, inline-rendered formatting via a real native text engine (TextKit 2), no web view, no lag.
1. **Sync that just works** — record-level CloudKit model, silent, account-scoped, conflict-aware, offline-first.
1. **Beautiful themes** — theming is a core feature, decoupled from storage. Switch in a tap or build your own.
1. **Tags & smart lists** — inline `#tags`, auto-built smart lists (Pinned, Today, Untagged, per-tag), zero setup.
1. **Instant search** — full-text index, compose tag + text filters (`#work invoice`).
1. **Never trapped** — one toggle mirrors every note to real `.md` files with YAML frontmatter — drop straight into Obsidian.

-----

## Voice & tone

- **Confident, not loud.** “Reliability over cleverness.”
- **Plain-spoken.** Short sentences. No jargon walls.
- **Honest.** “We never see a word you write.” “No subscription games.”
- **Crafted.** Word choice mirrors the product’s polish — every line earns its place.

Recurring rhetorical move: *name the trade-off everyone assumes, then dissolve it.* (“Everything a note app should be. Nothing it shouldn’t.”)
