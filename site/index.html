TextKit 2 powers live, themed rendering as you type. Headings, quotes, lists, code and inline tags style themselves inline — while the underlying file stays clean, portable Markdown.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know before you start writing in Husky Notes.

Is Husky Notes really free?

Yes. Husky Notes is free forever with every feature included — no subscriptions, no unlocks, no upsells. The complete app is free for everyone.

Which devices does Husky Notes support?

Husky Notes is native for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It requires iOS or iPadOS 18, or macOS 15 or later.

Where are my notes stored, and is Husky Notes private?

Your notes live in your own private iCloud database, scoped to your Apple ID and encrypted in transit and at rest. There's no Husky server and no account to create — we never see a word you write.

Can I export my notes or move them to another app?

Yes. Every note is plain Markdown. One toggle continuously mirrors your notes to real .md files with YAML frontmatter, so you can drop them straight into Obsidian or any editor. Your data is never trapped.

Is Husky Notes open source?

Yes. Husky Notes is fully open source under the MIT license. You can read the code, audit the sync, file issues, or build it yourself on GitHub.

Does Husky Notes work offline?

Yes. Husky Notes is offline-first — keep writing with no connection and it syncs automatically through iCloud when you're back online.
