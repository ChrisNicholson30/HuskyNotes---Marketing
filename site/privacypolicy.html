Privacy Policy

Husky Notes · Last updated: 6 June 2026

Husky Notes (“the app”, “we”, “us”, “our”) is a free, open-source Markdown note-taking app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app and this website do and do not collect, how it is handled, and your rights.

The short version: we never see your notes. Husky Notes has no servers, no accounts, and no analytics on your content. Your data is yours, forever.

1. Who we are

Husky Notes is designed and built by CN-DESIGN LTD (“the developer”). For any privacy questions, contact us at contact@huskynotes.com.

2. Your notes and your data

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to your notes.

  • All notes are stored locally on your device and, if you enable iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud database, scoped to your Apple ID.
  • Your notes are encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple's iCloud infrastructure.
  • There is no Husky Notes server. Your data never passes through, or is stored on, any system we operate or control.
  • There is no account to create. We do not ask for, collect, or hold any login credentials, email address, or identity information to use the app.
  • Sync is handled entirely between your devices and your private iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit. We cannot read, access, or recover your notes.

Because we have no access to your content, we cannot retrieve, restore, or delete notes on your behalf. Managing your notes and your iCloud data is entirely within your control.

3. Markdown export

Husky Notes can continuously mirror your notes to plain .md files (with YAML frontmatter) on your device or in your iCloud Drive, so you can use them in other apps such as Obsidian. These files are created and managed locally by you. We have no visibility into, or access to, exported files.

4. iCloud and Apple

Husky Notes uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) to sync your notes between your devices. This processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and iCloud terms, not by us. We receive no personal data from Apple about your iCloud usage or content. If you choose not to enable iCloud sync, your notes remain only on your device.

5. Optional device security

Husky Notes offers an optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple on your device. We never receive, store, or have access to your biometric data.

6. Analytics, tracking and advertising

  • Husky Notes contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs that monitor your content or behaviour inside the app.
  • We do not display advertising.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share personal information — because we do not collect it.
  • We do not use your notes for training machine-learning models or any other purpose.

We may receive anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics from Apple's App Analytics. This data is not linked to your identity and never includes the content of your notes. You can opt out in your device's Privacy settings under “Share With App Developers”.

7. This website (huskynotes.com)

This website is informational. We do not use cookies for tracking, and we do not run third-party advertising or analytics that profile you. Standard server logs may be retained by our hosting provider for security and operational purposes; these are not used to identify individual visitors.

If you contact us by email, we use your message and email address solely to respond to you, and retain it only as long as necessary for that purpose.

8. Children's privacy

Husky Notes is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. As the app collects no personal data, it can be used by people of any age in accordance with Apple's App Store terms.

9. Open source

Husky Notes is fully open source under the MIT license. You are welcome to read the code, audit how sync and storage work, and verify these privacy claims for yourself at github.com/ChrisNicholson30/HuskyNotes.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under data-protection laws such as the UK GDPR, EU GDPR, or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) — including rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you.

Because Husky Notes does not collect or hold any personal data about you, in most cases there is nothing for us to access, correct, or erase. Your notes are under your own control via your device and your iCloud account. If you believe we hold any personal information about you (for example, an email you sent us), contact us and we will address your request.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app or website after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
