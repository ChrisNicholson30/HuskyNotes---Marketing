Getting started

New to Husky Notes? Here's everything to get writing in a couple of minutes.

How do I install Husky Notes?

Download Husky Notes from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad, or from the Mac App Store on your Mac. It's free, with every feature included — no account or sign-up required.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no Husky Notes account, password, or login. To sync across devices, simply sign in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple ID — that's all the setup there is.

What are the system requirements?

Husky Notes is native for iPhone, iPad and Mac and requires iOS or iPadOS 18, or macOS 15 or later. Older versions aren't supported.

Sync & iCloud

Sync runs through your own private iCloud (CloudKit) — there's no Husky server in the middle. Most sync hiccups are quick to fix.

My notes aren't syncing between devices

Sync relies on iCloud, so work through these in order — it resolves the vast majority of cases:

  1. Make sure every device is signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID (Settings → your name on iOS; System Settings → your name on Mac).
  2. Check that iCloud Drive is turned on, and that iCloud is enabled for Husky Notes in your iCloud settings.
  3. Confirm each device has a working internet connection — sync pauses offline and resumes automatically when you're back online.
  4. Give it a moment. The first sync after install, or a large change, can take a little time to propagate.
  5. Fully quit and reopen Husky Notes on both devices to nudge a fresh sync.
  6. Check Apple's System Status page — if iCloud is having an outage, sync will catch up once it clears.

Still not syncing after all of that? Email us with your device and OS versions and we'll help dig in.

I signed in on a new device and my notes aren't there yet

On a fresh device, the initial download from iCloud can take a few minutes depending on how many notes you have and your connection. Make sure you're on the same Apple ID, keep the app open, and stay connected to Wi-Fi while it catches up.

Does Husky Notes work offline?

Yes — Husky Notes is offline-first. Keep writing with no connection at all, and your changes sync automatically through iCloud the next time you're online.

Where exactly are my notes stored?

In your own private iCloud database, scoped to your Apple ID and encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple. There's no Husky Notes server, and we can't read, access, or recover your notes. See our Privacy Policy for the full picture.

Notes, Markdown & editing

Everything is plain Markdown, rendered live as you type.

Which Markdown is supported?

Husky Notes uses a live, native Markdown editor supporting CommonMark and GitHub-Flavored Markdown (GFM) — headings, lists, links, code, tables, task lists and more, rendered as you write.

How do tags and lists work?

Add inline #tags anywhere in a note to organise and filter, and use smart lists that continue automatically as you type. Tags make it easy to group related notes without folders.

How do I search my notes?

Use the built-in instant full-text search to jump to any note by its content or tags. It searches across everything, including synced notes from your other devices.

Can I add images or attachments?

Yes. Husky Notes supports attachments and inline image handling, so you can keep visuals alongside your writing.

Export & .md files

Your notes are never locked in — they're real Markdown files you control.

How do I export my notes?

Turn on the continuous Markdown mirror to keep a live two-way set of plain .md files (with YAML frontmatter) on your device or in iCloud Drive. You can also export individual notes whenever you like — every note is plain text underneath.

Can I use my notes in Obsidian or another app?

Absolutely. Because the mirror produces standard Markdown with YAML frontmatter, you can point Obsidian (or any editor) straight at the folder. Edits flow both ways, so your data is never trapped.

Where are the exported files kept?

Wherever you choose — locally on your device or in your iCloud Drive. The files are created and managed by you; we have no visibility into or access to them.

Themes & appearance

How do I change the theme?

Husky Notes ships with beautiful built-in themes and a full theme editor, so you can fine-tune colours and typography or build your own. Light and dark modes are both supported and follow your system preference by default.

This website's light/dark mode

Use the sun/moon toggle in the top-right of any page to switch this site between light and dark. Your choice is remembered on your device.

Privacy, security & your data

Can you recover or restore notes for me?

Because we never have access to your content, we can't retrieve, restore, or delete notes on your behalf — your notes live entirely in your own iCloud account. Keeping iCloud enabled (and, optionally, the Markdown mirror) is the best way to make sure you always have a copy under your control.

Is there an app lock?

Yes. Husky Notes offers an optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple on your device — we never receive or store your biometric data.

How private is Husky Notes really?

We never see your notes. There are no servers we operate, no accounts, and no analytics on your content. You can read exactly how it works in our open-source code and in the Privacy Policy.
